# Dataset source

**Dataset:** Google Analytics 4 obfuscated sample e-commerce dataset

**Original public source:** https://developers.google.com/analytics/bigquery/web-ecommerce-demo-dataset

Google documents this as a public BigQuery sample of three months of obfuscated Google Merchandise Store event-export data. The repository uses the public dataset for analysis; the funnel definitions, SQL, and interpretation are independent portfolio work.
